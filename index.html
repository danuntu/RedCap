How to Use RedCap

1. Recording a Flow Test

Go to the Flow tab (or tap the Flow button on a hydrant's row in the Hydrants list). Pick the District, then type the hydrant number — a dropdown will suggest matching hydrants with their location, scoped to the selected district or across all districts if none is picked. Selecting one fills in the location automatically and shows the hydrant's last flow test and any tests already recorded this year. Fill in Date, Inspector, and pressures, then Calculate to get discharge and available fire flow, then Save. District and Inspector carry over to the next entry so you can move quickly hydrant to hydrant.

2. Recording an M&I

Same idea on the M&I tab. Each checklist and maintenance item is a three-way toggle — OK, Issue, or N/A — and marking something an Issue opens a note field for details. Saving updates that hydrant's status automatically based on what was found.

3. Finding a Hydrant

The Hydrants tab lists every hydrant with search, district, and status filters, and sortable columns. Tap a row to open its full detail page — specs, compliance badges, and complete history with its own year/month/search filters. From either the list or the detail page you can jump straight into recording a Flow Test or M&I for that hydrant.

4. Compliance Badges

A hydrant showing ⚠ No flow test in N years has gone 6+ years without a flow test that has real recorded readings — legacy "flowed and oiled" entries with no readings don't count. ⚠ Nothing recorded this year means no flow test or M&I has been logged for that hydrant so far this calendar year.

5. Reports

The Reports tab has one dropdown, grouped by Hydrants and Hoses, covering every report including Special Report Criteria (build your own hydrant query by type/status/date range/keyword) and Special Hose Criteria (filter hoses by size, color, service status, apparatus/location, or last-test date range, and optionally group into totals by size or apparatus instead of listing each hose). Picking a report that needs criteria opens a "Report Criteria" panel below the dropdown with just the fields that report uses — the district filter only shows for hydrant reports, since hoses aren't districted. Print, Export CSV, and Blank Form Builder are tucked under the ⋯ button next to Reset, which always stays visible.

6. Printing

Printable PDFs are available from several places: a full record sheet from any hydrant's detail page, a blank fillable flow-test form from the Flow tab, any report from the Reports toolbar, a custom blank form you design yourself (Blank Form Builder, in Reports), and the hydrant list itself — with the option to include each hydrant's full history, or just a date range or year.

7. Working Offline

If a Flow or M&I save fails because of a lost connection, it's kept on the device and retried automatically the next time the app loads with a connection.
